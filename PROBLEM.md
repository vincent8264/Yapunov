## Challenge
Sound Classification quietly solves a problem that a lot of families worry about: how do you know when an emergency is happening without a camera watching your every move?

Picture an elderly relative living alone whose family wants peace of mind (without a camera in the room) and without their audio being sent anywhere. A small device sits on a shelf and distinguishes between a handful of sound signatures as they happen: glass breaking, a smoke alarm going off, a loud fall-like thud, or an unfamiliar voice in the middle of the night.

A strong demo proves that three different sounds can be registered and instantly translated into a visual alert: this can be a series of icons on the LED matrix clear enough that the person doesn’t need hearing to understand them - bonus points if you can send a text or email so the family knows the moment something happens! The audio itself should never leave the room. Create a solution that not only registers and categorizes the sounds correctly, but also alerts the elderly individual visually with the LED matrix, and the family remotely.
